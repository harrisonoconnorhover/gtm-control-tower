Privacy notice

This notice explains how Hollis Consulting handles information submitted through this website.

What we collect

When you request a demo, we collect your first and last name, business email, company name, and phone number if you choose to provide it. The site also processes basic request information, including your IP address, to operate the service and limit spam.

How we use it

We use your details to respond to your inquiry, discuss relevant consulting services, and keep a record of our follow-up. Submitting this form does not subscribe you to a newsletter.

Where it goes

Cloudflare hosts this website. Our GTM Control Tower intake sends your inquiry to Salesforce, where company and contact information and follow-up activity are stored. These service providers process information as part of delivering the website and handling inquiries.

Storage and your choices

We keep inquiry information for follow-up and related business recordkeeping. You may ask to access, correct, or delete your information, or ask us to stop contacting you. Use the contact form to request a conversation about your information, or reply to a follow-up you receive from Hollis Consulting.

Website storage

This website does not use advertising or analytics trackers. A request reference is held in the current page while you submit, so retrying an interrupted request does not create duplicate records.
