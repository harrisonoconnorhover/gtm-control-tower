What this case demonstrates—and its limits

Development accounts: HubSpot STANDARD and Salesforce Developer Edition (IsSandbox=false), not formally provisioned sandbox accounts. No production customer work. Real APIs; no mocked CRM responses. Screenshots mask account and native record IDs.

Salesforce blocked the second Jordan during fixture setup. A one-record allowSave=true acknowledgement seeded that coworker; application writes kept allowSave=false.

Before the new guard, Salesforce rejected the stronger Priya probe with DUPLICATES_DETECTED but accepted sparse Jordan. That single test-created Jordan was deleted and absence verified. Control Tower now holds both cases before provider creation.

This fictional exercise does not measure matching accuracy. A possible match may be a different person; resolving that identity remains human work. No candidate is not proof of absence, and records can change after a snapshot. State and city are matching context, not fields synchronized by this import.
