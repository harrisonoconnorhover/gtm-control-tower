Review existing-record updates.

Handoff files permit updates only. An unmatched person is held. Pomade’s Ready status is source context, not proof of accuracy, a confirmed identity, or permission to write.

  1. Select and map up to 100 Pomade rows, then download the preview plan. Larger selections stop; rows are never silently omitted.
  2. In the self-hosted workspace, choose Import Pomade handoff, inspect every proposal, and choose Load in new saved workspace. Preview and loading make no CRM writes.
  3. Use a direct CRM connector to compare existing people, resolve review holds, select protected field changes, and explicitly execute the reviewed plan.
  4. Inspect the receipt, source context, read-only verification, and eligible rollback in saved runs.

Local and private hosted Pomade can each export a file; neither installation has to share an account or database with Control Tower. Pomade’s separate direct CRM workflow remains independent. Legacy handoffs label missing dates, installation details, status, and evidence as unknown. Evidence for a value that changes remains historical source context.

What this case demonstrates—and its limits

Development accounts: HubSpot STANDARD and Salesforce Developer Edition (IsSandbox=false), not formally provisioned sandbox accounts. No production customer work. Real APIs; no mocked CRM responses. Screenshots mask account and native record IDs.

Salesforce blocked the second Jordan during fixture setup. A one-record allowSave=true acknowledgement seeded that coworker; application writes kept allowSave=false.

Before the new guard, Salesforce rejected the stronger Priya probe with DUPLICATES_DETECTED but accepted sparse Jordan. That single test-created Jordan was deleted and absence verified. Control Tower now holds both cases before provider creation.

This fictional exercise does not measure matching accuracy. A possible match may be a different person; resolving that identity remains human work. No candidate is not proof of absence, and records can change after a snapshot. State and city are matching context, not fields synchronized by this import.
